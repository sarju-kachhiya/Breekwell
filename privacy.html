Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us, and we are dedicated to keeping your personal information safe. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the data you provide to us. This policy applies only to this website (“the Site”), operated by DailySweeps (“we,” “our,” or “us”). It does not apply to third-party websites linked from the Site.

1. Information We Collect

We gather personal information to process your requests for products and services available through the Site. Information you may provide includes:

  • Full name, address, phone number, email, and other details required to match you with carriers and agents for quotes.
  • Demographic data (e.g., gender, ZIP code, or other non-identifiable details).
  • Information from other sources (online or offline) that may be added to your account.

2. How We Use and Share Information

We share your information in the following cases:

  • Quote Requests: If you request a quote, we may share your details (name, address, phone, email, car information, etc.) with carriers, agents, lead aggregators, and service providers to process your request.
  • Aggregated or Non-Personal Data: We may share de-identified or combined user information with partners for advertising or analysis.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose data if required by law, subpoena, court order, or audits.
  • Business Transfers: In case of a merger, sale, or acquisition, your data may be transferred to the successor entity but will remain under this Privacy Policy.
  • Third-Party Collection: Some third-party companies may collect data via cookies, pixels, or other tracking tools when you use our Site or read our newsletters.

3. Cookies and IP Tracking

We use cookies to personalize your experience and improve load times. We may also track click-stream data and IP addresses to optimize performance and deliver targeted ads.

4. Emails and SMS

Email

If you sign up, your email may be shared with our mailing partner for relevant offers. You can unsubscribe anytime.

SMS

By opting in, you agree to receive texts (standard carrier charges may apply). We do not guarantee delivery and are not responsible for your device’s security settings.

5. Data Security

We limit employee access, use security measures (physical, electronic, and procedural), and comply with federal standards to protect your information.

6. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) Rights

California residents have the right to:

  • Know what data we collect and how it’s shared.
  • Opt out of the sale of personal information.
  • Request access, portability, or deletion of personal data.
  • Receive equal service even if rights are exercised.
  • Designate an authorized agent to act on their behalf.

To make a CCPA request, contact us at CCPA Privacy Rights Request or call 312-212-0044.

7. Categories of Data Collected

Identifiers: name, address, email, phone

Protected characteristics: date of birth, gender

Internet activity: browsing history, site interactions

8. Use of Data

We use your information to:

  • Fulfill your service requests.
  • Provide relevant promotions.
  • Improve the Site and services.
  • Meet legal obligations.
  • Prevent fraud and ensure safety.
  • Conduct research and development.

9. Sharing of Data

With your consent (or subject to opt-out rights), we may share information with affiliates, service providers, and marketing partners. We do not knowingly collect or sell data of users under 18.

10. Third-Party Links

Interactions with external websites linked on our Site are governed by their privacy policies.

11. Children’s Privacy

This Site is intended for users 18 years and older.

12. Removing Information

Some information may be legally required to be retained and cannot be deleted on request.

13. Updates to Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will appear on this page, and continued use of the Site indicates your acceptance of the revised policy.
